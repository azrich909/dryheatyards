Finding a licensed landscaper in Arizona

Whether you need landscapers in Phoenix, Tucson or a smaller town, Dry Heat Yards lists 244 ROC-licensed landscaping companies in 23 Arizona cities, from the West Valley and East Valley to Tucson, Yuma, Flagstaff, Prescott and Sedona. Pick your city above to compare Arizona landscaping companies side by side, with phone numbers, websites, services and Google ratings on one page.

What we check on every listing

  • License: each contractor is matched to an active Arizona Registrar of Contractors (ROC) license, and listings are re-checked monthly. How we verify
  • Ratings: star ratings come from each business's Google profile, not from us.
  • Labels: yard maintenance crews we could not match to a license are clearly marked as not ROC-verified.

Before you hire

Get two or three written quotes, confirm the license number on the ROC website, ask for proof of insurance, and check your HOA's approval rules before work starts.
