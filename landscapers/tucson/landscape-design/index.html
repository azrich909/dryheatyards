Frequently asked questions

How many licensed landscape design & installation contractors serve Tucson?

17 ROC-licensed contractors on this page offer landscape design & installation in Tucson. 4 of them hold our Top Rated 2026 award (Google 4.8+ from 100+ reviews). Each license was checked against the Arizona Registrar of Contractors list in November 2026.

How much does landscape design & installation cost in Tucson?

A full desert landscape install usually costs about $5 to $20 per square foot in Arizona. Simple rock-and-plants yards land near the bottom; pavers, turf and walls push it up. Details: cost guide. Get at least three written quotes.

What should I ask before hiring for landscape design & installation?

Ask for a written plan or drawing with a plant list, sizes and where drip lines go. Confirm the license class covers the work (landscape contractors usually hold CR-21, R-21 or C-21). Ask what happens if plants die in the first months, and get any plant warranty in writing. HOA? Get design approval before work starts, especially for front yards.

How do I check a contractor's license in Arizona?

Every licensed company on Dry Heat Yards shows its ROC license number. You can look it up on the Arizona Registrar of Contractors website to confirm it is active and see any complaints. How we verify.
