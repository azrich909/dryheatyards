Frequently asked questions

How many licensed landscapers serve Peoria?

This page lists 21 landscaping contractors serving Peoria with an active Arizona ROC license. We check every license against the state list and re-check monthly.

Does a landscaper need a license in Arizona?

Arizona generally requires a Registrar of Contractors (ROC) license for landscaping work of $1,000 or more, or any job that needs a permit. Routine mowing, trimming and cleanup crews usually don't need one, so we list them separately. Learn more.

How much does desert landscaping cost in Peoria?

Professional desert landscaping in Arizona usually costs about $5 to $20 per square foot, or roughly $5,000 to $20,000 for a 1,000 sq ft yard. See our cost guide, and get at least three written quotes.

Are there rebates for removing grass?

Many Arizona water providers offer grass removal and smart irrigation controller rebates. Check with your water company before you start. Rebate guide.
