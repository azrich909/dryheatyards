Weeds in Sun City: what to expect

Sun City was one of the first big retirement communities in the country, and most homes have low-water rock yards. Many residents spend summers away, and that's exactly when monsoon weeds come up and go to seed.

If you're gone for the summer, a company that treats your yard in late summer or early fall means you come back to a clean yard instead of a dried-out weed patch. Gravel yards here respond well to a regular pre-emergent schedule.

Frequently asked questions

When should I put down pre-emergent in Sun City?

Twice a year: September to October, and again February to early March. Water it in after you apply it. See the weed calendar.

Do weed control companies need a license in Arizona?

Yes. Companies that spray weed killer for hire are licensed by the Arizona Department of Agriculture, not the ROC. Every company on this page is matched to an active category 3 (Ornamental & Turf) license.

How many weed control companies serve Sun City?

This page lists 10 licensed weed control companies serving Sun City. We only list companies rated 4.0 or higher on Google from at least 25 reviews. Call to confirm they cover your address.

Why do weeds keep coming back in my rock yard?

Thin rock lets weeds through. Adding about 2 inches of rock helps, and it's easier to kill weeds while they're small. Our rock calculator shows how many tons to order.

What should I ask before hiring a weed control company?

Ask what they spray, how long pets should stay off the yard, and whether they offer pre-emergent in fall and late winter. Get 2 to 3 quotes.
