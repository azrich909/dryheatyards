What to ask the rock yard before you order

  • Delivery fee and minimum order. Many yards charge by distance and have a minimum number of tons for delivery.
  • Size and color in stock. Ask for the exact rock name and size (for example 1/2" or 3/4" screened) and look at a sample if you can.
  • Where they dump it. Trucks are heavy. Ask whether they can drop on your driveway or street and whether they put boards down.
  • Price per ton vs. per yard. Make sure you compare the same unit. The calculator below works in tons.
  • Pickup option. Some yards load your truck or trailer for small amounts, which can save the delivery fee.

Frequently asked questions

How much landscape rock do I need?

Rock is sold by the ton. Enter your yard size in our rock calculator to get the tons to order, with 10% extra built in, and a cost estimate.

Do rock yards deliver?

Many do. Delivery fees usually depend on distance, and many yards have a minimum number of tons for delivery. Some yards also load your truck or trailer for small amounts.

Is rock priced by the ton or by the yard?

It varies by yard, so make sure you compare the same unit. Ask for the exact rock name and size, and look at a sample if you can.

How do I keep weeds out of new rock?

Put down pre-emergent before or right after the rock goes in, and use about 2 inches of rock. See how to stop weeds in rock.
