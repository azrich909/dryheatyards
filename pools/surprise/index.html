Owning a pool in Surprise

Surprise has many pools in active-adult communities and newer neighborhoods. Seasonal residents often need a service that keeps the pool clean and balanced while they're away for the summer, which is exactly when pools need the most care.

Hot summers mean high evaporation and more chlorine use, and dust storms load up filters. If you're away for months, ask a company whether they'll send photos or reports after each visit.

Before you hire a pool company

Ask for the ROC license number and check it yourself, get a written contract, and ask who pulls the permit. Read our guide to hiring a pool company in Arizona.

Frequently asked questions

Do pool builders need a license in Arizona?

Yes. Pool builders and pool repair companies need an Arizona ROC license. We match each one to an active license. Pool cleaning services are listed separately and aren't ROC-verified.

How do I choose a pool company in Surprise?

Get 2 to 3 written quotes, confirm the license on the ROC site, and ask about warranty and insurance.
