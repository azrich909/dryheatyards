Owning a pool in Avondale

Avondale summers are long and very hot, so pools here often run the pump longer, lose a lot of water to evaporation, and go through more chlorine than in milder places. A good weekly service keeps up with that without overdoing the chemicals.

Like most of the Valley, Avondale has hard water, and calcium builds up as a white line on tile. Regular brushing and keeping the water balanced slows it down, and a tile cleaning every few years takes care of the rest.

Before you hire a pool company

Ask for the ROC license number and check it yourself, get a written contract, and ask who pulls the permit. Read our guide to hiring a pool company in Arizona.

Do pool builders need a license in Arizona?

Yes. Pool builders and pool repair companies need an Arizona ROC license. We match each one to an active license. Pool cleaning services are listed separately and aren't ROC-verified.

How do I choose a pool company in Avondale?

Get 2 to 3 written quotes, confirm the license on the ROC site, and ask about warranty and insurance.
