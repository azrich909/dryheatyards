Owning a pool in Scottsdale

Scottsdale has everything from older pools in south Scottsdale to large custom pools with water features in the north. Bigger projects like negative edges, spas and outdoor kitchens need a licensed builder, so check the ROC license and any HOA rules before you start.

Hard water and summer heat are the main challenges. Pools with lots of water features lose more water to evaporation, so a good service company should keep an eye on water levels in summer.

Before you hire a pool company

Ask for the ROC license number and check it yourself, get a written contract, and ask who pulls the permit. Read our guide to hiring a pool company in Arizona.

Do pool builders need a license in Arizona?

Yes. Pool builders and pool repair companies need an Arizona ROC license. We match each one to an active license. Pool cleaning services are listed separately and aren't ROC-verified.

How do I choose a pool company in Scottsdale?

Get 2 to 3 written quotes, confirm the license on the ROC site, and ask about warranty and insurance.
