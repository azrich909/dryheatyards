What to know before hiring for desert landscaping & xeriscape

Xeriscape, also called desert landscaping, uses desert-adapted plants, decorative rock and drip irrigation instead of thirsty grass. Done well, it cuts outdoor water use and still looks lush.

  • Ask whether your city offers a grass removal rebate, and whether the rebate needs approval before work starts.
  • Make sure the old grass (especially Bermuda) is killed first, or it grows back through the rock.
  • Plants should be spaced for their full-grown size, with trees and shrubs on separate drip zones.
  • Ask what weed control is included: pre-emergent, fabric, or both.

How much does xeriscape & desert landscaping cost in Arizona?

Professional desert landscaping in Arizona usually costs about $5 to $20 per square foot, or roughly $5,000 to $20,000 for a 1,000 sq ft yard. Details: cost guide. Get at least three written quotes.

How do I check a contractor's license in Arizona?

Every licensed company on Dry Heat Yards shows its ROC license number. You can look it up on the Arizona Registrar of Contractors website to confirm it is active and see any complaints. How we verify.

Is there a rebate for removing grass?

Several Arizona cities pay rebates for removing grass. Phoenix, for example, pays $2 per sq ft for removing at least 250 sq ft of live grass, with pre-approval required before any work. See rebates by city.
