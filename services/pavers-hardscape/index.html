What to know before hiring for pavers & hardscape

Hardscape is the built part of a yard: paver patios, walkways, driveways, walls, fire pits and outdoor kitchens.

  • Ask how deep the compacted base is and what edge restraint holds the pavers.
  • Patios should slope slightly away from the house so water drains off.
  • Retaining walls, gas lines and electrical work may need permits. Ask who pulls them.
  • Get the payment schedule in writing, and avoid paying most of the price up front.

What should I ask before hiring for pavers & hardscape?
