Irrigation & Drip Systems in Arizona

Drip and sprinkler installation, repairs, leak fixes, and smart controllers that save water. 71 ROC-licensed contractors, grouped by city.

What to know before hiring for irrigation

Irrigation covers drip systems, sprinklers, valves and controllers. Most Arizona water waste comes from leaks and controllers that never change with the seasons.

  • Trees, shrubs and grass need different watering, so they should be on separate zones or valves.
  • Ask about a smart controller and whether your city offers a rebate for one.
  • Ask whether a backflow device is required where you live.
  • After the job, have them walk you through the controller schedule for summer and winter.

Frequently asked questions

How many licensed irrigation & drip systems contractors serve Arizona?

71 ROC-licensed contractors on this page offer irrigation & drip systems in Arizona. Each license was checked against the Arizona Registrar of Contractors list in November 2026.

What should I ask before hiring for irrigation & drip systems?

Trees, shrubs and grass need different watering, so they should be on separate zones or valves. Ask about a smart controller and whether your city offers a rebate for one. Ask whether a backflow device is required where you live. After the job, have them walk you through the controller schedule for summer and winter.

How do I check a contractor's license in Arizona?

Every licensed company on Dry Heat Yards shows its ROC license number. You can look it up on the Arizona Registrar of Contractors website to confirm it is active and see any complaints. How we verify.

Can I get a rebate for a smart irrigation controller?

Some Arizona cities offer rebates for smart controllers or grass removal, and rules differ by city. Check our rebates by city guide and confirm with your water provider before you buy.
