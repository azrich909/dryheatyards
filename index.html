Weeds in your rock yard

If you have a rock yard in the Valley, you know how it goes: weeds after the winter rains, then again after the monsoon storms.

What actually works is pretty simple. Put down a pre-emergent in the fall and again in late winter, and water it in. Pull or spray whatever still comes up while it's small. And keep enough rock on the ground. A lot of Valley homes were built with a thin layer, so adding a couple of inches on top helps more than most people expect.

Frequently asked questions

Is Dry Heat Yards free to use?

Yes, it's free for homeowners. You see each company's phone number and call them yourself. No forms, no lead fees and no middleman.

How do you check the companies you list?

Every contractor is matched to an active Arizona Registrar of Contractors (ROC) license and re-checked monthly. Weed control companies are matched to an active Arizona Department of Agriculture license. How we verify.

Do I need a licensed landscaper?

Arizona generally requires a Registrar of Contractors (ROC) license for landscaping work of $1,000 or more, or any job that needs a permit. Routine mowing, trimming and cleanup crews usually don't need one, so we list them separately. Learn more.

What is a Featured listing?

Featured listings are paid placements at the top of a city page, and they're always labeled. Basic listings are free and always will be.
