First-month checklist

  • Find your irrigation controller and check the schedule. Many new homes are set to water too often.
  • Walk the yard at night with a UV flashlight to see if scorpions are around.
  • Pull weeds after the first rain, while the soil is soft.
  • Read our new-home backyard guide before you plan any big projects.

FAQ

What yard tools do I need for a rock yard?

A rake that will not pull up the rock, a weed puller, pruners for shrubs and a soil moisture meter cover most rock-yard upkeep. A leaf blower helps with litter from trees like palo verde.

Why is a UV flashlight on a yard tool list?

Scorpions glow under ultraviolet light, so a UV flashlight is the easiest way to check walls, patios and garages at night.

Do I need a lawn mower in Arizona?

Only if you have grass. Many Arizona yards are all rock and desert plants. If you have a small Bermuda strip, a cordless string trimmer and edger may be enough.
