Desert garden basics

  • Raised beds filled with good soil skip caliche entirely.
  • Test soil before adding amendments.
  • Cover citrus and tender plants on nights forecast near freezing.
  • See our desert plants guide for what to plant.

FAQ

Why use raised beds in Arizona?

Raised beds let you garden in good imported soil above hard native soil and caliche.

Is Arizona soil alkaline?

Many Arizona soils are alkaline and low in organic matter. A soil test tells you what your yard needs.

When do I need frost cloth in Arizona?

On winter nights forecast near freezing, mainly for citrus and frost-tender plants. Remove it in the morning.
