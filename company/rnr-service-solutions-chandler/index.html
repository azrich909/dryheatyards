What the CR-6 license covers

CR-6 (Swimming Pool Service and Repair) is a dual (residential and commercial) Arizona ROC license. It covers service and repair of pools, spas and pool equipment for homes and commercial properties. It does not cover building a new pool.

Summary of the license scope in the Arizona Administrative Code (R4-9-102 to R4-9-104). Ask the contractor whether your specific job falls under their license.
